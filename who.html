// WHO IS WUBU

A small studio. One person.

Thoughtful, useful and occasionally playful software for Apple platforms. The kind of apps you actually want to keep.

The story

WuBu, LLC is a small independent software studio focused on thoughtful, useful and occasionally playful software for Apple platforms. We build the kinds of apps we want to use ourselves, tools that get out of your way and games that respect your time.

What we make

The work falls into two simple buckets:

  • macOS utilities that sharpen everyday workflows for power users and makers.
  • iPhone games built with care, playable in short bursts, without ads or dark patterns.

How we work

We ship slowly and deliberately. Polish before features. Done before deadlines we made up.

We prefer polished releases over feature creep, and clear pricing over data collection. If something feels off, we pull it before you do.

Get in touch

Questions, feedback or an idea you want to see built? Reach out to us@wubu.ai. Every message is read.
