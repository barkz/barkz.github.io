// Clocktastic

Time where you see it.

An easy-to-use macOS desktop clock that floats on your desktop as a transparent clock. Set focus time with a countdown and reminder, check the weather at a glance and customize the look to suit you.

Floating clock

A transparent clock sits above every window. No more digging for the menu bar.

World times

See San Francisco, New York, London and Tokyo side by side. Plan calls across time zones without math.

Focus countdown

Set a focus block. Clocktastic counts down and sends a reminder when time is up.

Weather at a glance

Current conditions live on the clock face. One look covers time and weather.

Your look

Adjust opacity, size and style until the clock fits your desktop and your colour scheme.
