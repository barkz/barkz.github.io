// FROM THE DESK

Putting a small thing into the App Store has been a quiet dream of mine for a long time. Not for downloads or charts. For the simple feeling of here, I made this.

WuBu is one person, working slowly, building three apps that fit into the corners of life. A clock. A puzzle. A note that travels.
