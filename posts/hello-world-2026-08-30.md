# Hello, world

This is the first note from the WuBu desk. There is no schedule and no
content calendar. When something is worth writing down — a decision we
argued about, a bug that taught us something, a release that finally
landed — it will show up here.

## What this is for

Mostly it is a build log. We are two people making Mac utilities and
iPhone games slowly, and most of what we learn along the way disappears
into a chat window. Writing it down forces us to know why we did things.

Expect posts about:

- **Decisions we reversed**, and what it cost to reverse them.
- **Small Mac details** — window behaviour, menu bar manners, the things
  you only notice when they are wrong.
- **Release notes with actual reasons** behind the changes.

## How this blog works

Every post is a markdown file in `posts/`, named for its topic and date:

```
posts/hello-world-2026-08-30.md
```

No database, no build step, no CMS. Write the file, run the index script,
push. If you are the kind of person who enjoys that sentence, you will
probably enjoy the apps too.

> The list is short on purpose, and it moves at the speed two people can
> move without hating it.

More soon. Probably not that soon.
